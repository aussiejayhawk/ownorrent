Versus renting: invest the difference

Holding costs = interest + council rates + body corporate. Principal repaid is equity, not a cost. Growth is compounded annually; council and body corporate held flat; rent rises once a year. Renting: each month, owning cash out (repayment + council + body corp) minus rent is invested at your return rate, compounded monthly. If rent is higher, the pot draws down. Investment returns are pre-tax. Inputs are saved in this browser only (localStorage), never sent anywhere. Estimate only, not financial advice.
